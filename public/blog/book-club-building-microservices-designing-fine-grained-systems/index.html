Announcing Our New Book Club!

We are excited to launch a new book club dedicated to continuous learning and professional growth.

Our Mission

Our primary goals for this book club are:

  • To Learn New Things: We aim to explore new concepts and technologies together, fostering a collaborative learning environment.
  • To Build Consistency: By meeting regularly, we can create a consistent habit of reading, learning, and sharing knowledge.

How It Works

  • Weekly Virtual Meetings: We will host a weekly meeting on Google Meet/Zoom to discuss the designated chapter.
